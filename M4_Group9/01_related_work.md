# Related Work and Literature Review

The literature review is organised around three themes relevant to the project: scattering-parameter modelling of RIS networks; reciprocal BD-RIS design under symmetric-unitary constraints; and optimisation methods for group-connected architectures.

## Scattering-network modelling
Shen, Clerckx, and Murch (IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications, 2022), “Modeling and Architecture Design of Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces Using Scattering Parameter Network Analysis,” is relevant to representing RIS architectures through scattering matrices and network constraints. Before finalising this discussion, verify the precise assumptions and claims in the full paper, including its losslessness and reciprocity treatment.

## Reciprocal BD-RIS and manifold optimisation
The project SOTA notes identify arXiv:2607.22170, arXiv:2604.22469, and arXiv:2511.07683 as relevant recent works on reciprocal/group-connected BD-RIS and symmetric-unitary optimisation. Their exact algorithms, constraint sets, convergence claims, and comparison settings must be verified from the full PDFs. Abstract-only reading is insufficient for a detailed technical claim.

## Comparison framework
For every paper, extract: architecture/connectivity; scattering-matrix constraints; channel/system assumptions; objective; fixed versus jointly optimised precoder; optimisation algorithm; convergence evidence; and whether reciprocal and general designs are compared under matched conditions.
